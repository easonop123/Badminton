# Cover Design Concept (make it free in Canva)

You don't need design skills. Open **canva.com** (free), search the template type **"Kindle Cover"** or **"Ebook Cover"** (1410 x 2250 px works well), and recreate this:

## Layout (top to bottom)
1. **Background:** a deep green-to-dark gradient (matches the guide's colours), OR a slightly darkened action photo of a badminton player lunging/jumping. Canva has free badminton stock photos — search "badminton".
2. **Small top label:** `BADMINTON PERFORMANCE GUIDE` — small, uppercase, light grey, letter-spaced.
3. **Big title:** `THE OFF-COURT EDGE` — large, bold, white. This is the hero element.
4. **Subtitle:** `Training, Recovery & Performance Beyond the Court` — medium, light green/white.
5. **Feature strip near bottom:** six small words or icons in a row:
   `Strength · Plyometrics · Agility · Nutrition · Sleep · Mental Game`
6. **Optional bottom:** your name or "By [your name]" to build your personal brand.

## Colour palette (paste these hex codes into Canva)
- Dark green: `#0b3d2e`
- Bright accent green: `#16a34a`
- Off-white text: `#f6faf8`
- Muted grey label: `#94a3b8`

## Font pairing (Canva free fonts)
- Title: a strong bold font like **Anton**, **Bebas Neue**, or **Archivo Black**
- Body/subtitle: a clean font like **Poppins** or **Montserrat**

## Tips
- Keep the title readable as a thumbnail — that's how people see it first on Gumroad.
- High contrast (light text on dark background) sells best.
- Export as PNG and upload it as the product cover.

Takes about 10–15 minutes. Done once, used forever.
