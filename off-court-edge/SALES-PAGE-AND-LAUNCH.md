## 5. FAQ section (add to the listing — reduces hesitation)

**Do I need a gym?**
No. Most of the program uses bodyweight, a resistance band, and optional dumbbells. Gym options are included if you have access.

**I'm a beginner — is this too advanced?**
No. Everything starts at a beginner level with clear progressions as you improve.

**How long are the workouts?**
Most sessions are 15–30 minutes. There's a minimum plan for as little as 2–3 hours a week.

**What format is it?**
An instant-download PDF you can read on your phone, tablet, or print out.

**Is this medical advice?**
No — it's general educational guidance. The guide includes a disclaimer and recommends checking with a professional before starting, especially if you're under 18 or have an injury.

## 6. Step-by-step launch plan

### Setup (one time, ~30–45 min)
1. Create a free account at **Gumroad.com** (or **Ko-fi.com** — both let you sell digital downloads for free; they take a small cut per sale).
2. Connect a payout method (PayPal or your NZ bank — this is the part only you can do).
3. Create a new product → upload the **PDF** (made from the HTML — see below).
4. Paste in the **title**, **description**, **FAQ**, and set the **price** to NZ$7.
5. Add a cover image (see the cover concept file / make one free in Canva).
6. Hit publish and copy your product link.

### Turn the guide into a PDF (2 min)
1. Open `The-Off-Court-Edge.html` in any web browser.
2. Press **Ctrl/Cmd + P** (Print).
3. Choose **"Save as PDF"** as the destination → Save.
4. That PDF is your product file to upload.

### Get your first sales (the part that actually earns)
Post your link where badminton players gather. Lead with value, not just "buy my thing":

- **Reddit:** r/badminton (huge + active). Share a genuinely useful tip from the guide, then mention the full guide in a comment/your profile. Read each sub's self-promo rules first.
- **Facebook groups:** search "badminton" + your city/region, plus NZ badminton groups. Many allow occasional sharing.
- **Your own club:** noticeboard, group chat, clubmates. Warm audience = your easiest first sales.
- **TikTok / Instagram / YouTube Shorts:** short clips of a drill or tip from the guide → link in bio. This is the channel that can keep selling for you long-term.
- **Discord:** badminton communities.

### Keep it selling (low ongoing effort)
- Pin a couple of helpful posts; the links keep working after you stop posting.
- Ask early buyers for a quick review/testimonial — social proof drives more sales.
- Every few weeks, post one new tip clip. That's the only "ongoing work," and it's optional.
