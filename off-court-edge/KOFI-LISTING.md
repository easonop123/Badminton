## FAQ (add to the bottom of the description)

**Who's it for?**
Players who already play and train and want to break a plateau — club, league and competitive players. Keen beginners get a lot from it too; it just assumes you want the deeper "why," not a starter routine.

**Do I need a gym?**
No. Most of it uses bodyweight, a band, a medicine ball and optional dumbbells. Gym alternatives are included.

**What format is it?**
An instant-download PDF — read it on your phone, tablet, or print it out.

**Is this medical advice?**
No — it's general educational guidance and includes a clear disclaimer. Check with a professional before starting if you're under 18, injured, or unsure.
