## 1. Reddit — r/badminton (value-first post)

**Title:** The off-court stuff that improved my game more than any amount of extra hitting

**Body:**
For years I just turned up and hit shuttles, and kept losing to people I was technically better than. What actually changed my game was the off-court work I'd been ignoring. A few things that made the biggest difference:

- **Smash power isn't in the wrist.** It's forearm rotation (pronation) with a relaxed grip that only snaps tight at contact. Trying to "flick" harder just gave me a sore elbow.
- **Train braking, not just speed.** Most knee pain in badminton is from absorbing deep lunges. Slow eccentric split squats + isometric holds (Spanish squat) did more for my knees than any stretch.
- **Stop jogging for fitness.** Badminton is short max efforts with short rests. Interval ghosting (10s on / 20s off) transfers way better than steady running.
- **Read the hall drift in the knock-up.** Same clear carries deep one end and drops short the other. Free points once you notice it.

Happy to go deeper on any of these in the comments. I ended up putting everything I learned into a proper guide — link's in my profile/below if it's useful, but genuinely happy to just chat technique here too.
