# Ko-fi Setup Guide — Get "The Off-Court Edge" Live in ~20 Minutes

A simple, no-skip walkthrough to go from zero to a live, sellable product link.
Everything here is the part only **you** can do (it needs your real identity + a payout account).

---

## Before you start — have these ready
- Your **PDF** (open `The-Off-Court-Edge.html` in a browser → Print → Save as PDF).
- A **cover image** (optional but recommended — see `COVER-CONCEPT.md`, made free in Canva).
- A **PayPal account** (easiest in NZ) **or** a card/bank for Stripe.
- The sales copy from `SALES-PAGE-AND-LAUNCH.md` (title, description, FAQ).

---

## Step 1 — Create your Ko-fi account (3 min)
1. Go to **ko-fi.com** and click **Sign up**.
2. Use your email (or Google). Pick a page name — this becomes your link, e.g. `ko-fi.com/benbadminton`.
   - Tip: pick something badminton-y and memorable. You'll share this link everywhere.
3. Add a profile photo and a one-line bio (e.g. *"Off-court training for badminton players. Move faster, last longer, win more."*).

## Step 2 — Connect how you get paid (5 min)
1. Go to **Settings → Payments**.
2. Connect **PayPal** (recommended for NZ — fastest) and/or **Stripe**.
   - PayPal: log in and authorise. Done.
   - Stripe: follow the prompts (name, address, NZ bank account).
3. This is the bit that makes the money actually reach you, so don't skip it.

## Step 3 — Turn on your Shop (2 min)
1. In your dashboard, find **Shop** (or **Settings → Shop**) and enable it.
2. Ko-fi's shop is free; it just takes a small 5% fee per sale (plus normal card fees).

## Step 4 — Add the product (6 min)
1. Click **Add a new item** → choose **Digital product / Digital download**.
2. **Upload your PDF.** Ko-fi delivers it automatically to buyers after payment.
3. Fill in the listing using `SALES-PAGE-AND-LAUNCH.md`:
   - **Title:** The Off-Court Edge — The Badminton Player's Guide to Training, Recovery & Performance Off the Court
   - **Price:** NZ$9 (or set "pay what you want" with NZ$9 minimum)
   - **Description:** paste the sales description
   - **Cover image:** upload your Canva cover
4. (Optional) Add the **FAQ** text to the bottom of the description.
5. Hit **Save / Publish**.

## Step 5 — Test it yourself (3 min)
1. Open your shop link in a private/incognito window so you see what buyers see.
2. (Optional but smart) Buy your own copy with a different email to confirm the **PDF actually delivers**. You can refund yourself after.
3. Check the cover, price, and description all look right.

## Step 6 — Copy your link and launch
1. Copy your product/shop link.
2. Use the posts in `LAUNCH-POSTS.md` to share it (Reddit, Instagram, your club, Facebook groups).

---

## Quick troubleshooting
- **"I'm not getting paid out"** → Re-check Settings → Payments; make sure PayPal/Stripe is fully connected and verified.
- **"Buyers can't download"** → Confirm the item type is *Digital download* and the file uploaded fully.
- **Tax/GST?** → At your scale this is usually minimal, but keep a record of sales. If you ever earn a lot, check NZ IRD guidance or ask an accountant. (General info, not tax advice.)
